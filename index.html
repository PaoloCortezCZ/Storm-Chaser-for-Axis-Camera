Point your camera at the storm — automatically.

Storm Chaser is a CamScripter microapp for Axis IP cameras. It scans the weather around the camera, finds the strongest cell, and aims at it via PTZ preset, CamSwitcher view, or continuous pan / tilt / zoom.

Three ways to track

Pick the mode that fits your hardware — switch any time.

PTZ presetsAssign up to 8 of the camera's presets to compass directions; jump to the nearest one.
CamSwitcher viewsSwitch between cameras / view areas per direction via CamSwitcher.
PTZ trackingContinuous absolute pan / tilt / zoom — zoom scales with storm distance.

What it does

Everything runs on the camera; the map & tuning live in the settings UI.

🌩 Storm scoring

Ranks cells from free Open-Meteo data — lightning potential, CAPE, precipitation and wind gusts, with adjustable weights.

🛰 Always-on data

Falls back to keyless MET Norway when Open-Meteo's daily quota is hit, so tracking never stops.

🌪 NWS alerts (US)

Optionally follows live tornado / severe thunderstorm / hurricane warnings from the National Weather Service.

🗺 Live map

Camera, scan radius, colored sectors, RainViewer radar, and red storm dots. Click a dot to lock on manually.

🖥 CamOverlay output

Burn live status to an InfoTicker line and/or Custom Graphics fields.

⚙️ Tuneable

Metric / Imperial, 360° / 180° coverage, dwell & hysteresis anti-jitter, export / import, light & dark UI.

Get started

Three steps from zip to chasing storms.

  1. Install CamScripter on your Axis camera (ARTPEC-6 or newer).
  2. Upload storm_chaser_3_0_1.zip via CamScripter → Add package.
  3. Open the settings UI, set camera, location and a targeting mode, then Save & restart.

Full detail: the bundled UserGuide.md or the illustrated Operations guide (PDF).
